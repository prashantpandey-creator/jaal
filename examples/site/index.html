This page has no server.

It lives on someone's own computer and reached you across the net, node to node. There's no hosting company and no domain registrar, and no one in the middle can change a word of it.

  • Its address is a key. The 32 letters in the bar are a hash of its owner's public key. Nobody issues it, so nobody can take it away.
  • Every byte is signed. Your node checked each file against the owner's signed list before showing it to you. A relay that altered anything would have been caught.
  • It outlives its owner's laptop lid. Any node can choose to keep a copy alive. When the owner is offline, the mirrors serve it, and they can't forge it either.
