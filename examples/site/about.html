Indra's net

In the old image, Indra's net hangs a jewel at every knot, and each jewel reflects all the others. Jāla (जाल) means net.

  • Links. Nodes connect over anything that carries bytes: the same Wi-Fi with no internet at all, the internet itself, a VPN. Every link is encrypted and both ends prove who they are.
  • Routes. Each node announces itself, signed, and the announcement spreads. Every node learns which neighbour leads toward everyone else.
  • Sealed packets. What you send is locked to the recipient's key. Relays carry it, but they can't read it.
  • The bridge. A node with internet can share it as an exit. Set your browser's proxy to your own node and the whole regular internet travels across Jāla. HTTPS stays encrypted end to end, so the exit sees only ciphertext.
